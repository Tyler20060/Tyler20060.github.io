Millions of people with colour blindness live full, successful lives. With the right strategies, tools, and support, colour blindness doesn't have to limit your opportunities.

Daily Strategies

At Work

💼 Workplace Strategies:

Communication:
  • Tell your manager about your colour blindness
  • Ask colleagues to use labels for color-coded items
  • Request written instructions instead of color-only information
  • Suggest accessibility improvements for everyone

At School

🎓 Education Strategies:

For Students with Colour Blindness:
  • Inform teachers about your colour blindness
  • Ask for printed materials with alternative colors or patterns
  • Request access to digital versions of materials
  • Use note-taking strategies to supplement color-based information

At Home

🏠 Home Organization:

Organization Tips:
  • Label household items clearly (especially medication)
  • Use shapes and patterns for organization
  • Keep consistent organization systems
  • Use texture for distinction (smooth vs bumpy containers)

Professional Careers

Careers That May Be Restricted

⚠️ Professions with Color Vision Requirements:

Some professions require standard color vision for safety:
  • Aviation: Commercial pilots need color vision for signal lights
  • Military: Many combat roles require color vision
  • Electrical work: Wire color identification critical
  • Medicine: Some specialties require color vision (diagnostics)

Successful Careers for Colour-Blind Professionals

✅ Career Options:

Thousands of successful professionals have colour blindness:
  • Software development and IT
  • Engineering (structural, mechanical, civil)
  • Teaching and education
  • Law and business
  • Finance and accounting
  • Research and science
  • Manufacturing and production
  • And many others!

Helpful Tools and Technologies

Smartphone Apps

📱 Useful Apps:

Color Detection:
  • Color Blind Pal: Identifies and describes colors
  • Seeing AI: AI assistant that describes visual content
  • Color Identifier: Use phone camera to detect colors

Computer Tools

💻 Computer Accessibility:

Operating System Features:
  • Windows: High Contrast Mode, Settings → Ease of Access
  • Mac: System Preferences → Accessibility → Display
  • Linux: Various accessibility settings available
Third-Party Software:
  • f.lux: Adjusts screen colors based on time of day
  • Color Oracle: Simulates colour blindness to test designs
  • Windows Magnifier: Built-in magnification tool

Key Takeaways

  • ✅ Colour blindness is manageable with proper strategies and support
  • ✅ Communicate with others about what you need
  • ✅ Use available tools - apps, software, and devices
  • ✅ Many successful careers are available to colour-blind professionals
  • ✅ Your attitude matters - most colour-blind people live full, happy lives
