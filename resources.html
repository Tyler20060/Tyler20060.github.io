## Testing and Diagnosis ### Color Vision Tests
🧪 Testing Resources:

Ishihara Test (Most Common):
  • 38 color-coded plates with numbers or patterns
  • Free online version available
  • Quick and simple screening
  • Limited diagnostic detail
Farnsworth-Munsell 100 Hue Test:
  • Arrange colored caps in order of hue
  • More detailed than Ishihara
  • Professional-grade testing
  • Available at eye clinics
Anomaloscope:
  • Most accurate color vision test
  • Requires specialist equipment
  • Available at major eye centers
  • Gold standard for diagnosis
### Where to Get Tested
Testing Locations:

Eye Care Professionals:
  • Optometrist's office
  • Ophthalmologist's office
  • Local vision centers
Online Testing:
  • Free online Ishihara test
  • Approximate screening only
  • Professional testing recommended
Genetic Testing:
  • Genetic counseling centers
  • Hospital genetics departments
  • Direct-to-consumer genetic testing

## Legal and Employment Rights ### Anti-Discrimination Laws
⚖️ Your Legal Rights:

United States:
  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) - Protects workers
  • Section 508 - Requires accessible federal technology
  • WCAG standards - Web accessibility guidelines
United Kingdom:
  • Equality Act 2010 - Disability protections
  • Public Sector Equality Duty
  • Workplace accommodations required
Canada:
  • Canadian Human Rights Act
  • Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act
  • Workplace accommodation requirements
Australia:
  • Disability Discrimination Act
  • Australian Human Rights Commission
### Workplace Accommodations
💼 Your Rights at Work:

You May Be Entitled To:
  • Accessible formatting of documents (non-color-dependent)
  • Modified job tasks where appropriate
  • Technology aids (screen readers, magnification)
  • Time for adjustments and training
  • Alternative communication methods
Steps to Take:
  1. Document your colour blindness diagnosis
  2. Inform HR or your manager in writing
  3. Request specific accommodations needed
  4. Follow company procedures for accommodation requests
  5. Keep records of all communications
